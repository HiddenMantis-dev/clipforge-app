Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

CLIPFORGE ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what it does with data from connected platforms such as TikTok.

What we receive

  • From TikTok Login Kit: your basic profile (open id, display name, avatar) and access / refresh tokens, with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  • From the Content Posting API: the status and id of the videos the app publishes for you.

How it is used

Only to show which account is connected and to publish the videos and captions you approved, at the time you chose. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable by the owner. We run no server that collects your data, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Videos are sent only to the platform you chose to publish on.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting the account in the app deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (Manage app permissions).

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or deletion requests: open an issue.
