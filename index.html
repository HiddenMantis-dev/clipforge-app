CLIPFORGE

CLIPFORGE is a private desktop application that the owner uses to edit and publish short-form videos for their own brand accounts. It turns long videos into vertical clips (captions, framing, music), and schedules them on the owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit: the owner signs in to their own TikTok account to connect it to CLIPFORGE.
  • Content Posting API: at the time the owner scheduled, CLIPFORGE uploads the finished video with its caption to the owner's account (to the TikTok inbox as a draft, or posted directly once approved).
  • CLIPFORGE only posts videos that the owner has reviewed and approved. It does not read, follow, like, comment or message on anyone's behalf.

CLIPFORGE is not a public service: it runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to other users.
